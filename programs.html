Four engineering services, all under the responsible charge of a licensed Florida PE.

Program 01 · What is visible, stated as such

Structural assessment

Owners usually want a yes or no, and the honest answer is a report that distinguishes what was observed, what it indicates and what could not be seen. Concealed framing, undocumented past alterations and latent defects are limits of any visual assessment, and they belong in the report rather than being implied away.

Program 02 · Loads actually run

Design & calculations

Removing a wall, adding a storey or hanging new equipment changes the load path, and in Florida wind loading governs more than people expect. Calculations are performed and documented, so the design can be defended to a plans examiner rather than asserted.

Program 03 · Signed, dated and sealed

Permit drawings

Permit drawings are prepared to what the local authority actually requires, which varies between jurisdictions more than most people expect. We respond to plan review comments as part of the work — a set that collects comments and is then abandoned helps nobody.

Program 04 · Before and after

Inspections & post-storm

After a storm, owners and insurers both need an independent engineering opinion on what is damage and what was already there. We assess and report factually. We do not adjust claims, we do not represent anyone in a dispute, and we will not characterise pre-existing conditions as storm damage.
